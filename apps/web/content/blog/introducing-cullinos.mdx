We're building **Cullinos** to solve a problem every restaurant operator knows: too many disconnected tools for POS, kitchen, ordering, inventory, and reporting.

## One platform, every channel

Cullinos brings your front of house, kitchen, customer ordering, and back office onto a single cloud backend. Orders from the cashier, waiter app, QR menu, and online storefront all flow through one unified order engine.

## Built for India

From day one, Cullinos is designed for Indian restaurants:

- **GST billing** with CGST, SGST, and IGST
- **INR** pricing and Asia/Kolkata timezone defaults
- **Offline POS and KDS** via the Local Gateway when connectivity drops

## Who it's for

Whether you run a single outlet, a growing chain, or F&B inside a hotel, Cullinos scales with subscription plans from Starter to Hospitality.
