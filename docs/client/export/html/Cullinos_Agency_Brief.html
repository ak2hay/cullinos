2 · The problem we solve

A typical Indian restaurant runs on a patchwork: a basic billing machine, paper kitchen tickets that get lost, separate Swiggy and Zomato tablets, Excel for stock, WhatsApp groups for staff, and nothing (or another tool) for loyalty and customer data. Every tool has its own menu, prices, and login — and nothing talks to anything else.

Cullinos replaces the patchwork with one live system. Change a price once and it updates on the POS, QR menu, kiosk, and Guest app. An order from any channel appears on the kitchen screen instantly. Sales, stock, and customers live in one dashboard.

9 · Brand messaging kit

Positioning statement

For Indian food businesses tired of juggling separate billing, kitchen, delivery, and marketing tools, Cullinos is the restaurant operating system that runs every role and every order channel on one live platform, with GST billing built in. Unlike a basic POS, Cullinos connects cashier, kitchen, floor, guests, and owner — from one outlet to a hotel chain.

Key messages

  1. One system, every service moment. Billing, kitchen, floor, online, and back office, connected.
  2. Every order reaches one kitchen. POS, waiter, QR, app, and aggregator orders on the same screen.
  3. Built for India. GST invoices, UPI payments, SMS and WhatsApp, phone OTP.
  4. Each role gets its own app. Cashier, chef, and waiter each see only what they need.
  5. Grows with you. From a food truck to a franchise network, on the same platform.

Tagline options

Your food business operating system. One system. Every service moment. Every order channel. One kitchen. From one outlet to hotel chains — same platform.

Tone of voice

  • Confident, practical, warm — like an experienced restaurant manager, not a software salesperson.
  • Show the moment, not the module — "the grill sees the order the second it's placed", not "KDS with station routing".
  • India-first — rupee prices, UPI, GST, lunch-rush and chai-break examples.
  • Plain language — explain jargon (see the glossary) or avoid it.

12 · Do's and don'ts

Do

  • Show real service moments (rush hour, table QR, kitchen screen) rather than abstract dashboards.
  • Say "GST-ready billing with CGST, SGST, and IGST".
  • Say Swiggy and Zomato orders come into Cullinos alongside your own orders.
  • Mention that the Guest app is on Android.
  • Send any new claim, statistic, or customer quote to Rkyves for approval before publishing.

13 · Glossary

TermMeaning
POSPoint of Sale — the billing screen at the counter
KOTKitchen Order Ticket — the order slip sent to the kitchen
KDSKitchen Display System — a kitchen screen that replaces paper KOTs
CDSCustomer / Order Display — the "Preparing / Ready" board
QSRQuick Service Restaurant — counter service, cafes, fast food
Cloud kitchenA delivery-only kitchen, often running several brands
Central kitchenOne kitchen preparing food or stock for several outlets
AggregatorThird-party delivery apps like Swiggy and Zomato
GST · CGST / SGST / IGSTIndia's Goods and Services Tax and its central, state, and integrated parts
GSTINA business's GST registration number
HSN / SACTax classification codes printed on invoices
ModifierAn add-on or change to an item ("extra cheese")
Table sessionAn open table bill that waiter and guests can both add to
Folio · Room postingA hotel guest's room bill · charging F&B to it
BanquetA large event with set menus and billing
IndentA stock request from an outlet to a central kitchen
PO / GRNPurchase Order / Goods Receipt Note
ShiftA cashier's session, with cash counted at open and close
Deep linkA link that opens a specific restaurant in the app or web
OTP · UPI · DPDPOne-time password · India's instant payment system · India's data protection law
